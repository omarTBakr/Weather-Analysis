\textbf{PM Accelerator — Mission}\\[0.4em]
PM Accelerator (Dr.\ Nancy Li) is a global community that helps aspiring and current product managers
fast-track their careers through coaching, an AI Product Management bootcamp and hands-on product experience.
Through its non-profit arm \emph{PMA Kids}, it offers free product-management education to teenagers from
underserved families:\\[0.4em]
\emph{``Our mission is to break down financial barriers and achieve educational fairness. With the goal of
establishing 200 schools worldwide over the next 20 years, we aim to empower more kids for a better future
in their life and career, simultaneously fostering a diverse landscape in the tech industry.''}\\[0.3em]
{\small Source: \url{https://www.pmaccelerator.io/}}
